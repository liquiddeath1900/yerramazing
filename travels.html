How I keep it moving

Travel doesn't have to wreck your progress. Here's how I stay on track wherever I land.

Train anywhere
Hotel room, beach, a park, a stairwell. Bands and bodyweight do the job — no gym required. The workout travels with me, every trip.
Eat smart on the go
New food everywhere — I still hit protein and stay close to my targets without missing the local spots. Enjoy the trip, don't blow the progress.
Explore + move
Steps add up. Walking a new city, hiking, swimming — the trip itself becomes the cardio. Movement is the default, not a chore.
Document it real
The wins and the off-days, no filter. If I can stay consistent across time zones, so can you. That's the whole point.
