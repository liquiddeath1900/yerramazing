Her Coaching

Tonight's restart workout + activation cues for every exercise. The cues tell you what to feel on every rep — that's what makes light weight build muscle.

Tonight: Restart / Re-Entry Day

Two parts: warm-up, then workout. Total ~35-40 min. Walk out feeling worked, not wrecked. After 2-3 sessions like this she goes back to the regular A/B/C rotation in Her Plan.
Warm-Up · ~10 min
Mat → activation → cardio
1. Quick mat stretch · 1-2 min
Hip flexors, hamstrings, low back. Whatever feels tight from sitting at the desk all day. Don't overthink it.
2. Dead Bugs · 1 set × 8 reps each side
Slow tempo. Low back glued to the floor. This wakes up the deep core before anything else loads.
3. Hip Thrust · 1 set × 10 reps, very light
Smith machine or bench-and-bar, just the bar — no plates. Or floor glute bridge if Smith is parked. The point is to wake up the glutes, not lift weight.
4. Treadmill OR Stair Master · 5 min, easy pace
Easy walking pace on the treadmill, or low-level on the stair master. Goal is core temperature up + a light sweat starting. Not a cardio session.
Workout · ~25 min
4 moves · 7 working sets total
Low load tonight. If she finishes feeling like she could've done more — that's correct. Banking the energy is the point.
1. Floor Glute Bridge · 2 sets × 10 reps
Load: bodyweight only.
Tempo: 2-sec squeeze at the top.
Feel: both glutes squeezing hard at the top — like clenching to hold a coin.
Rest: 60 sec between sets.
Full activation card →
2. Leg Press · 2 sets × 10 reps
Load: sled empty OR 1 plate per side MAX.
Feet: shoulder-width, mid-platform.
Feel: quads loading on the way down, glutes assist at lockout.
Rest: 90 sec between sets.
Plan B if parked: goblet squat to a box, 10-15 lb DB, 2 × 10.
Full activation card →
3. Cable Glute Kickback · 2 sets × 10 reps each leg
Load: light — ankle cuff, zero grip needed.
Feel: top/back of glute squeezing the leg backward and slightly up.
Rest: 60 sec between sets.
Plan B if parked: standing banded kickback, 2 × 10/leg.
Full activation card →
4. Dead Bug · 1 set × 8 reps each side
Load: bodyweight, slow tempo.
Feel: deep abs locked, low back GLUED to the floor.
Stop if: low back arches off the floor.
Full activation card →
Done. 5-min easy walk before getting in the car. Next session in ~48 hr — same warm-up, same 4 moves, slightly more load if everything felt clean. After 2-3 of these she's back on the regular A/B/C rotation.

Activation Cues — Every Exercise

At the weights she'll use for the next 6-8 weeks, what she feels matters more than what she lifts. Schoenfeld 2018 showed that consciously focusing on the target muscle increases its activation by 22-26% at submaximal loads (≤60% 1RM). Translation: if she can't feel it where the card says, the rep didn't count. Drop the weight, slow down, find the muscle, then build back up. This is the literal mechanism that makes light weight build muscle.
Card format: Setup (machine config) → Target (which muscle) → Feel HERE (the cue) → NOT here (the leak + fix) → Self-check (a physical test) → Breath (the pattern). Pull up the relevant card on her phone between sets if a move feels off.
Glutes
Glutes
Floor Glute Bridge Watch →
Setup: floor on back, knees bent, heels under knees, arms relaxed at sides
Target: glutes (max), hamstrings secondary
Feel HERE: hard squeeze across both cheeks at the top — like clenching to hold a coin between them
NOT here: if low back arches (ribs flaring up), drop ribs, tuck pelvis under, lift less high but squeeze harder
Self-check: hand on glute mid-rep — should feel rock-hard contraction at top, soft at the bottom
Breath: inhale on the way down, exhale powerfully through the drive up
Glutes
Single-Leg Glute Bridge Watch →
Setup: same as bridge, lift one foot off floor (knee bent at 90°), grounded heel under knee
Target: glute of grounded leg (heavier dose than two-leg version)
Feel HERE: one side of the glute lighting up sharply
NOT here: if low back does the lift, the pelvis is tilted forward — tuck pelvis under hard before pushing up
Self-check: hips stay level — no dipping toward the lifted side (camera/mirror confirms)
Breath: exhale up, inhale down
Glutes
Hip Thrust (Smith Machine) Watch →
Setup: bench at shoulder-blade level, bar on hip pad, feet flat shoulder-width, hands rest on bar (no grip)
Target: glutes (max + medius), hamstrings
Feel HERE: top of glutes crushing the bar straight up — not forward, not back
NOT here: if quads do the work, feet are too far away — walk feet in until knees stack over ankles at the top
Self-check: chin tucked, ribs down, body forms a flat tabletop hip-to-knee at the top, 2-sec pause
Breath: inhale at the bottom, brace, exhale through the heel-driven drive up
Glutes — deferred
Barbell Hip Thrust (session 2-3, not tonight) Watch →
Setup: bench at shoulder-blade level, padded bar on hip crease, feet flat hip-width
Target: glutes, hamstrings
Feel HERE: same as Smith — glutes drive the bar to a clean horizontal lockout
NOT here: if low back hyper-extends at top (ribs flared), finish with ribs DOWN and glutes UP — not the other way around
Self-check: at the top, body forms a flat tabletop hip-to-knee, knees stacked over ankles
Breath: inhale at the bottom, brace, exhale through the drive
Glutes
Cable Glute Kickback Watch →
Setup: ankle cuff on working leg, cable low, light weight, free hand rests on machine for balance only
Target: glute max, especially the upper portion (the "shelf")
Feel HERE: top/back of glute squeezing the leg backward and slightly up
NOT here: if low back arches to lift the leg higher, the spine is doing the work — let the glute, not the spine, raise the leg; smaller range, harder squeeze
Self-check: free hand on working glute — feel the contraction at the top of every rep
Breath: exhale on the kick, inhale on the controlled return
Glutes — medius
Banded Hip Abduction (Seated) Watch →
Setup: seated on bench, band looped above knees, feet flat shoulder-width
Target: glute medius (upper/outer glute — the shelf-look muscle)
Feel HERE: burning sharply at the side of each hip, just below the iliac crest
NOT here: if quads brace instead of glutes pushing, sit upright and don't lean back; the push is OUT, not down
Self-check: push knees out, 2-sec hold, slow controlled return — no bouncing
Breath: exhale on the push out, inhale on the return
Glutes — hinge
Cable Pull-Through Watch →
Setup: rope between legs at low pulley, feet shoulder-width, slight knee bend, face away from stack
Target: glutes + hamstrings (hinge pattern)
Feel HERE: deep stretch in the back of the thighs at the bottom, glutes lock out at the top
NOT here: if she squats instead of hinges, push hips BACK toward an imaginary wall behind her; knees barely bend
Self-check: rope arrives at hips, not at chest; ribs stay down at lockout
Breath: inhale on the hinge back, exhale on the drive forward
Glutes — deferred
Bulgarian Split Squat (session 2-3) Watch →
Setup: rear foot elevated on bench laces-down, front foot 2-3 ft forward, slight forward torso lean
Target: front-leg glute, quad secondary
Feel HERE: front glute stretch at the bottom, full glute contraction at the top
NOT here: if pressure piles into the front knee, step the front foot further forward + lean torso slightly more
Self-check: front shin stays roughly vertical at the bottom; back knee lightly grazes floor
Breath: inhale down, exhale up, brace through the entire rep
Quads / Hams / Calves / Adductors
Quads + Glutes
Leg Press Watch →
Setup: back flat against pad, feet shoulder-width on mid-platform, slight outward toe
Target: quads + glutes
Feel HERE: quads loading on the way down, glutes assisting at the lockout
NOT here: if low back rounds off the pad at the bottom, stop short of that depth — that's where her safe range ends
Self-check: knees track in line with toes — never caving inward
Breath: inhale on the way down, exhale on the press up
Quads + Glutes
Goblet Squat Watch →
Setup: hold one DB at chest, elbows tucked, feet shoulder-width, slight outward toe
Target: quads, glutes, core
Feel HERE: quads on the way down, glutes through the drive up, core bracing the entire time
NOT here: if heels lift off the floor, stance is too narrow — widen and push knees out over toes
Self-check: chest stays up, weight stays in heels, hips drop straight down
Breath: inhale at top, brace, exhale on the drive up
Quads
Leg Extension Watch →
Setup: knees aligned with machine pivot, ankle pad just above the foot, back firmly against pad
Target: quads (all four heads)
Feel HERE: sharp squeeze across the top of the thigh at lockout — 2-sec hold
NOT here: if low back pulls off the pad to drive harder, drop the weight and squeeze harder
Self-check: toes pulled up toward shin biases quads over hip flexors
Breath: exhale on the lift, inhale on the slow controlled return
Hamstrings
Seated Leg Curl Watch →
Setup: pad just above heels (Achilles), thigh pad firm, knees at machine pivot
Target: hamstrings
Feel HERE: back of the thigh contracting hard as heels pull down/under
NOT here: if glutes lift off the seat to muscle the weight, drop the load and slow the tempo
Self-check: toes flexed up toward shin (more hamstring), thighs stay pinned to seat
Breath: exhale on the curl down, inhale on the slow 3-sec eccentric
Hamstrings + Glutes
Lying Leg Curl Watch →
Setup: face-down, knees just off the bench edge, ankle pad on Achilles
Target: hamstrings + glute lower fibers
Feel HERE: hamstrings squeezing as heels pull toward butt
NOT here: if hips lift off the bench, pin pelvis to the bench and use a lighter load
Self-check: toes flexed up, slow 3-sec eccentric on every rep
Breath: exhale on the curl, inhale on the return
Hams + Glutes — deferred
Romanian Deadlift (DB) (session 2-3) Watch →
Setup: DBs in front of thighs (with straps for grip), feet hip-width, soft knees
Target: hamstrings + glutes
Feel HERE: deep stretch in the back of the thighs as DBs slide down to mid-shin
NOT here: if low back rounds, bend more from the hips and less from the spine; stop the descent when hams are stretched, not when DBs hit the floor
Self-check: DBs travel close to the legs, neutral spine, slight chin tuck
Breath: inhale on the hinge down, exhale on the drive up
Quads or Glutes
Walking Lunges Watch →
Setup: long stride bias glutes, short stride bias quads; torso upright; hands on hips or light DBs at sides
Target: glutes (long stride) or quads (short stride)
Feel HERE: glute of the front leg stretching at the bottom, glute driving the stand-up
NOT here: if knee shoots past toes, the stride is too short — step longer, drop the back knee straight down
Self-check: back knee lightly grazes the floor each rep, torso stays upright
Breath: inhale on the descent, exhale on the drive up
Calves
Calf Raises (Seated + Standing) Watch →
Setup: seated = pad on quads, ball of foot on platform / standing = shoulder pad, ball of foot on edge
Target: soleus (seated, knees bent) / gastrocnemius (standing, knees straight)
Feel HERE: burn deep in the calf at the top, full stretch at the bottom
NOT here: if she's bouncing through reps, pause 1-sec at bottom and 1-sec at top
Self-check: big toe drives the lift (not the pinky-side)
Breath: exhale on the rise, inhale on the slow descent
Adductors
Adductor Machine Watch →
Setup: pads inside the knees, seat fully back, feet supported on the platforms
Target: inner thighs (adductors)
Feel HERE: burn in the inner thighs as knees squeeze toward each other
NOT here: if abs cramp, exhale fully on the squeeze — brace abs but don't grip them
Self-check: 1-sec hold at full squeeze, controlled 2-sec on the open
Breath: exhale on the squeeze in, inhale on the release
Upper Push
Chest + Tris
Machine Chest Press Watch →
Setup: seat height = handles at mid-chest, neutral grip handles, wrist wraps on
Target: chest (pec major), triceps secondary
Feel HERE: chest stretching as the handles move out, chest squeezing as the handles meet
NOT here: if shoulders roll forward, chest is hidden — chest up, shoulder blades pinned down and back into the pad
Self-check: 1-sec pause at full extension to feel the chest contraction
Breath: inhale on the way back, exhale on the press out
Chest
Cable Chest Fly Watch →
Setup: forearm cuffs (no grip needed) or very light open-palm hold, cable mid-height, slight forward lean
Target: chest (sternal head)
Feel HERE: stretch across the chest at the open, chest squeezing handles together at the close
NOT here: if elbows lock or overstretch, soften the elbow bend — elbows lead the arc, not the hands
Self-check: hands meet in front of the sternum, 2-sec squeeze at the close
Breath: inhale on the open, exhale on the close
Shoulders
DB Overhead Press (Neutral Grip) Watch →
Setup: seated against upright bench, DBs at shoulder height, palms facing each other (hammer)
Target: front delts, triceps secondary
Feel HERE: shoulders pressing the DBs straight up
NOT here: if low back arches to push heavier, brace abs hard, slight chin tuck, ribs DOWN
Self-check: at the top, DBs sit over the ears, not over the forehead
Breath: inhale at shoulder, exhale on the press up
Upper Pull
Lats
Lat Pulldown (Neutral Grip) Watch →
Setup: neutral-grip attachment, knees pinned under pad, slight backward torso lean, Versa Gripps or straps on
Target: lats (sides of upper back)
Feel HERE: sides of upper back pulling the elbows down toward the hips
NOT here: if biceps do the work, elbows lead the movement (not the hands), and the grip stays relaxed
Self-check: bar to upper chest, 1-sec squeeze, slow 2-sec eccentric
Breath: exhale on the pull, inhale on the return
Lats — isolation
Cable Straight-Arm Pulldown Watch →
Setup: tall stance facing high pulley, rope or straight bar, arms long with soft elbow bend
Target: lats (isolation — biceps stay quiet)
Feel HERE: lats squeezing as straight arms drive the bar down to the thighs
NOT here: if elbows bend during the pull, lock the soft bend — the movement happens at the shoulder, not the elbow
Self-check: slight forward hinge at hips, ribs down, bar finishes at thighs
Breath: exhale on the pull down, inhale on the controlled rise
Mid-back
Seated Cable Row (V-Bar) Watch →
Setup: V-bar attachment, knees softly bent, sit upright, neutral spine, straps on
Target: mid-back (rhomboids, mid-traps), lats secondary
Feel HERE: shoulder blades sliding back and squeezing toward each other
NOT here: if torso swings back to add momentum, ribs stay over hips — elbows do the work
Self-check: bar arrives at lower ribs, 1-sec squeeze, slow return
Breath: exhale on the pull, inhale on the return
Mid-back
Chest-Supported Machine Row Watch →
Setup: chest pinned to pad, neutral grip handles, palm-rest pad if available, light weight first
Target: mid-back, lats
Feel HERE: shoulder blades retracting, mid-back pulling elbows back behind the torso
NOT here: if hands grip hard, palms rest in handles and fingers stay relaxed — elbows drive, hands carry
Self-check: 1-sec pause with elbows behind torso every rep
Breath: exhale on the row, inhale on the return
Rear delts + posture
Face Pulls (Rope) Watch →
Setup: rope at face height, arms extended, light weight, split rope at the end of pull
Target: rear delts, mid-traps, rotator cuff
Feel HERE: back of the shoulders + the space between the shoulder blades
NOT here: if biceps load up, elbows lead high and wide — relaxed grip on the rope
Self-check: at the end, hands frame the face — thumbs by ears, elbows up and out
Breath: exhale on the pull, inhale on the return
Rear Delt / Posture
Rear delts
Rear Delt Machine Watch →
Setup: chest on pad, neutral grip handles, light weight first
Target: rear delts
Feel HERE: sharp squeeze at the back of the shoulders as elbows fan out and back
NOT here: if mid-back rows instead of rear-delt opens, think "open arms wide" not "pull elbows back"
Self-check: 1-sec pause at full extension, slow controlled return
Breath: exhale on the open, inhale on the return
Posture
Band Pull-Aparts Watch →
Setup: light band held at shoulder height with both hands, arms straight
Target: rear delts, mid-traps, postural muscles
Feel HERE: shoulder blades pinching together as the band stretches across the chest
NOT here: if shoulders shrug up, depress shoulders — ribs down, wrists straight
Self-check: slow tempo — 2-sec out, 2-sec in
Breath: exhale on the pull, inhale on the return
Arms
Biceps
EZ-Bar Curls Watch →
Setup: angled grip on EZ-bar (wrist-friendlier than straight), elbows pinned to ribs, wrist wraps on
Target: biceps
Feel HERE: biceps belly (middle of upper arm) doing the lift
NOT here: if wrists hyperextend, lighten the load and keep wrist in line with forearm
Self-check: elbows stay pinned to ribs (don't drift forward), 1-sec squeeze at the top
Breath: exhale on the curl up, inhale on the descent
Triceps
Tricep Pushdowns (Rope) Watch →
Setup: rope at high pulley, elbows pinned to ribs, light grip on the rope (fingers relaxed)
Target: triceps
Feel HERE: back of upper arm extending the rope down and apart
NOT here: if shoulders roll forward, pin shoulders back — only forearms move
Self-check: at the bottom, split the rope outward toward the thighs for full extension
Breath: exhale on the press down, inhale on the controlled rise
Core
Deep abs
Dead Bugs Watch →
Setup: floor on back, arms straight up, knees over hips at 90°, low back glued to floor
Target: deep abs (transverse abdominis), anti-extension control
Feel HERE: abs bracing tight as opposite arm and leg extend slowly
NOT here: if low back arches off the floor, stop and reset — only extend as far as the back stays glued
Self-check: slide a hand under low back — should feel zero gap throughout the rep
Breath: exhale on the extension, inhale on the return
Whole-body brace
Forearm Plank Watch →
Setup: elbows under shoulders, forearms flat, feet hip-width
Target: whole-body anti-extension brace
Feel HERE: glutes squeezing, abs gripping, quads tight — the whole body is one straight line
NOT here: if hips sag or pike, squeeze glutes harder and tilt pelvis under
Self-check: head, shoulders, hips, ankles in a straight line (mirror or partner check)
Breath: steady nasal breathing — never hold breath
Anti-rotation
Pallof Press Watch →
Setup: cable at chest height, stand sideways to the stack, hands gripped at chest, feet shoulder-width
Target: anti-rotation core (obliques + transverse)
Feel HERE: deep abs locking the body in place as the cable tries to twist her
NOT here: if body rotates toward the cable, step further away or drop the load
Self-check: press hands straight out from chest, hold 2-sec, return — body stays square the whole time
Breath: exhale on the press out, inhale on the return
Lower abs
Reverse Crunches Watch →
Setup: floor on back, knees bent over hips, hands at sides or under low back for support
Target: lower abs
Feel HERE: lower abs lifting hips off the floor — pelvis tilts under, knees move toward chest
NOT here: if momentum swings legs, slow it down and shrink the range — control the descent
Self-check: hips actually leave the floor on every rep (it's a hip lift, not a leg swing)
Breath: exhale on the lift, inhale on the lower
Obliques + abs
Bicycle Crunches Watch →
Setup: floor on back, hands LIGHTLY behind head (not pulling neck), knees up
Target: obliques + abs
Feel HERE: side of the abs as elbow drives toward opposite knee
NOT here: if she's pulling on the neck, hands rest only — lift comes from the abs, not the arms
Self-check: slow tempo (2 sec per side), full extension on the straightening leg
Breath: exhale on the crunch toward, inhale on the open
Lower abs
Hanging Knee Raises Watch →
Setup: ab straps on bar (zero grip demand — elbows in straps), body hanging straight
Target: lower abs
Feel HERE: lower abs curling the pelvis up as knees come toward chest
NOT here: if she's only swinging legs forward (hip flexion only), tilt pelvis under at the top — that's the rep
Self-check: at the top, low back rounds slightly — pelvis tilts posteriorly, not flat or arched
Breath: exhale on the curl up, inhale on the slow lower
Obliques — rotation
Cable Woodchops Watch →
Setup: cable high on one side, both hands on handle, wide stance, cable starts up by shoulder
Target: obliques (rotational core)
Feel HERE: side of the abs rotating the torso as hands chop diagonally to opposite hip
NOT here: if arms do all the work, hands stay long — pivot from the torso, back foot pivots with rotation
Self-check: hips and shoulders rotate together, eyes follow the hands
Breath: exhale on the chop, inhale on the return
Cervical / Wrist Rehab
Deep neck flexors
Chin Tucks Watch →
Setup: stand or sit upright, eyes level, jaw relaxed
Target: deep neck flexors (decompresses cervical spine)
Feel HERE: mild stretch at the base of the skull as the chin pulls straight back
NOT here: if she's tilting the head down, the chin moves backward (not downward) — like making a double chin
Self-check: hold 5-sec, ear stays over shoulder (not in front of it)
Breath: inhale before the tuck, exhale through the hold
Nerve mobility
Cervical Nerve Glides Watch →
Setup: standing, affected arm extended out to the side, palm up
Target: median nerve mobility (cervical to wrist)
Feel HERE: mild stretch sliding from neck through forearm — never sharp
NOT here: if it tingles, burns, or goes numb, back off immediately and shrink the range
Self-check: tilt head AWAY from the working arm + open/close hand together
Breath: steady, slow, no holding
Lower traps
Prone Y-Raises Watch →
Setup: face-down on incline bench, light DBs or no weight, thumbs up, arms in a Y
Target: lower traps, postural muscles
Feel HERE: the muscles between the shoulder blades pulling the arms up
NOT here: if upper traps shrug, shoulders DOWN — lift comes from below the shoulder blade, not above
Self-check: thumbs up the whole time, slow 2-sec lift, slow 2-sec lower
Breath: exhale on the lift, inhale on the lower
CTS rehab
Median Nerve Glides + Tendon Glides (combined) Watch →
Setup: done at home or in the warm-up; one hand at a time, full sequence in the main plan's wrist rehab section
Target: median nerve gliding through the carpal tunnel + tendon excursion
Feel HERE: smooth, light pulling sensation along the forearm as positions flow
NOT here: sharp tingling, burning, or numbness = the move is too aggressive — back off, smaller range
Self-check: 5-10 reps each hand; 2-3x daily speeds recovery (Cochrane review on conservative CTS care)
Breath: slow, relaxed, breath continues through every position

Target: glute medius (upper/outer glute)
Feel HERE: sharp burn at the top of the side of the working glute as the knee opens

Pattern check
Bodyweight Squat (Pattern Check) Watch →
Setup: feet shoulder-width, slight outward toe, arms forward for balance
Target: quad/glute coordination + ankle/hip mobility
Feel HERE: even split between quads working and glutes engaging at the bottom
NOT here: if heels lift or knees cave, widen stance and push knees out over toes
Self-check: hips below knees if mobility allows; if not, that's the data — work on hip mobility on rest days
Breath: inhale down, exhale up
How to use this page on the gym floor: when a move feels off, pull this page up between sets, find the card, read the "Feel HERE" + "NOT here" lines, then redo the next set with that focus. Schoenfeld's research shows the activation gain shows up in the very next set — not the next session.
