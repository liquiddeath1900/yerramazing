Her Rest Day

At the gym while he trains, but today is recovery — not a workout. Three blocks, ~35 min, walk out feeling looser than you walked in.
Why this isn't a workout: hypertrophy goal needs recovery between resistance sessions. HIIT or hard cardio today would stack fatigue and blunt the muscle-building stimulus from yesterday (Wilson 2012 meta, J Strength Cond Res — concurrent training interference). Light blood-flow movement actually clears soreness faster than sitting still (Dupuy 2018 meta, Frontiers in Physiology). So this is the right call — feels productive, doesn't cost recovery.

Block 1: Easy Walk-In

~10 min
Treadmill walk
Treadmill · 10 min
Pace: 3.0-3.2 mph.
Incline: 0%. No incline games today.
RPE: 2/10 — you can hold a full conversation, no breath break.
Goal: get warm, blood moving. That's it. Phone in hand is fine.

Block 2: Stepper + Mat Flow

Two halves, 10 min each. Stepper first (low impact, gentle glute pump), then mat (mobility + foam roll). No weights. No grip work. If anything starts feeling like a workout, slow down.
Half A · ~10 min
Stair stepper (or recumbent bike)
Stepper · 10 min
Level: 1-3 (lowest you can stand).
Pace: conversational — RPE ≤ 3/10.
Hands: rest on rails, no death-grip (wrists).
Plan B if knees feel fussy: recumbent bike, easy tension, same 10 min.
What you should NOT feel: heavy breathing, burning legs. If that shows up, drop the level.
Half B · ~10 min
Mat flow · mobility + foam roll
Move through the list once, slowly. No reps to chase. If something feels stiff, give it an extra minute. If something feels good, move on.
1. Cat-Cow · 10 reps slow
Hands and knees. Inhale, drop belly, eyes up. Exhale, round spine, eyes to thighs. Slow tempo — 4 sec each direction.
2. 90/90 Hip Switches · 10 each side
Sit on floor, one shin in front, other shin behind, both knees at 90°. Switch sides slowly, keeping torso tall. Opens up rotators after yesterday's hip thrust + glute work.
3. World's Greatest Stretch · 5 each side
From a low lunge, drop the inside elbow toward the floor near the front foot, then rotate the same-side arm up to the ceiling. Slow. This is the single best move for hips + thoracic mobility.
4. Glute Bridge Holds (bodyweight only) · 3 × 20 sec
This is NOT a hip thrust. No bar, no plates, no thrust pad. Just lift hips, squeeze glutes for 20 sec, lower. Recovery dose, not training dose.
5. Foam Roll · 3-5 min
Quads, glutes, lats. ~30 sec each side, slow passes.
Skip the forearms — wrist-protective rule.
Wiewelhove 2019 meta: short-term soreness reduction is real, the deeper "release" claims aren't — but the soreness reduction alone makes it worth the 5 min.

Block 3: Done

~5 min
Walk it out
Cooldown walk · 5 min
Treadmill 2.5-3.0 mph, 0% incline. Or a slow lap around the gym floor while he finishes.
Hydrate. Electrolytes if you sweat at all.
Tomorrow: real rest OR next restart session if it's been ~48 hr since the last one (check Her Coaching).
Done. The point of this page is to not turn rest day into stealth volume. If you finish and feel like you could've done more — perfect, that's the goal. Banking energy is the win.
Rule of thumb: if anything in here starts feeling like work — heavy breathing, burning legs, grip fatigue — slow down or skip the rest. Recovery isn't productive. That's the whole point.
