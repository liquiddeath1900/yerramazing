Her Plan

Full body every session — glutes, thighs, upper body, and core mixed together. Every workout includes glute work (3x/week frequency). Only 2-3 upper body exercises per session so wrists never get overloaded. 3-day base, optional 4th day.

The science: Women at 38 can gain 8-12 lbs of lean muscle in year one. Estrogen is still anabolic. Glutes respond best to high frequency — hitting them 3x/week (every session) beats 2x/week. Upper body is spread across all 3 days so wrists only handle 2-3 exercises at a time instead of 7.

Why Full Body 3x/Week?

Better for the goals AND the wrists. Schoenfeld's research: 2-3x/week frequency per muscle is optimal. Full body 3x hits glutes every session. And instead of one brutal upper body day (7 exercises beating up wrists), each session has just 2-3 upper body moves. If wrists flare mid-workout, skip those 2 exercises and keep going — the rest of the workout is still there.

Carpal Tunnel Gear

Every upper body exercise is CT-modified. Neutral grips, straps for pulling, wraps for pressing. No heavy barbell gripping, no wrist curls, no power cleans.
GEAR — WHAT TO BUY
Versa Gripps FIT — the single best investment. Replaces straps AND gloves. Shifts weight to the base of the hand, completely bypassing the carpal tunnel. Designed for smaller hands. Quick on/off. Works for pulling AND pushing. ~$45-55 on Amazon (4.6 stars, thousands of reviews)

Gymreapers Wrist Wraps (Stiff, 18") — locks the wrist neutral during bench and overhead press, prevents the hyperextension that aggravates CTS. IPF approved, lifetime warranty. ~$15-20 on Amazon (4.7 stars)

Copper Compression Fingerless Gloves — wear under wraps or alone during warm-ups. Mild compression reduces swelling in the carpal tunnel area, increases blood flow. Amazon bestseller, 50K+ reviews. ~$10-15

Gymreapers Lifting Hooks — backup if Versa Gripps aren't available. Steel hooks rated for 600+ lbs, neoprene wrist padding. ~$25-30 on Amazon (4.5 stars)

Fat grips — attach to dumbbells for curls (spreads load across palm)

Night splints — wear every night (neutral position, 0-15° extension). Not gym gear, but critical for recovery. Wraps ≠ splints. Wraps are gym-only (compress wrist for pressing). At night, use rigid neutral-position splints — separate product, ~$20.
Minimum starter kit: Versa Gripps FIT + Gymreapers Wrist Wraps + Compression Gloves = ~$75 total. Covers every exercise in this plan.

Workout L — Lower Body & Core Focus

Swap this in for any A/B/C session when you want to skip upper body entirely. Zero arm work, zero wrist load. All legs, glutes, and abs — higher volume than a normal day. This one builds the foundation for everything that comes next.
Lower — Glutes (Heavy)
Barbell Hip Thrust (Pad) — 4 × 8-10 Watch →
Bar on hip pad, hands guide only. Go as heavy as you can with good form. 2-sec squeeze at top. This is the main lift — earn it.
Lower — Quads
Leg Press — 4 × 10-12 Watch →
Feet shoulder-width, mid-platform. Push through full foot. Add weight each set if form holds.
Lower — Hams + Glutes
Romanian Deadlift (DBs + Straps) — 3 × 10-12 Watch →
Straps handle the grip. Hinge at hips, feel the deep hamstring stretch. Slow on the way down (3 sec).
Lower — Glute Isolation
Cable Kickbacks — 3 × 12-15 each leg Watch →
Ankle cuff — zero grip. Squeeze at the top. Don't swing, control every rep.
Lower — Inner/Outer Thigh
Adductor + Abductor Machine (superset) — 3 × 15 each Watch →
Superset — do adductors (squeeze in), immediately do abductors (push out). No rest between. 60 sec rest after both.
Lower — Quads (Burnout)
Walking Lunges (Bodyweight or Light DBs) — 3 × 12 each leg Watch →
Long stride = more glute. Short stride = more quad. Either works. Keep torso upright. These should burn.
Lower — Calves
Standing Calf Raise (Machine) — 3 × 15-20 Watch →
Full stretch at bottom, hard squeeze at top. No bouncing.
Core — Abs Circuit (3 rounds)
Abs Finisher — no rest between exercises, 60 sec between rounds
1. Reverse Crunches — 15 reps Watch →
Lie flat, lift hips off the floor. Lower abs focus. Control it — don't use momentum.

2. Dead Bugs — 12 each side Watch →
Back pressed into floor. Opposite arm + leg extend. Zero wrist load.

3. Forearm Plank — 30-45 sec Watch →
Squeeze everything — glutes, abs, quads. Don't sag, don't pike.

4. Bicycle Crunches — 15 each side Watch →
Slow and deliberate. Elbow to opposite knee, full extension on the other leg. Obliques + lower abs.
This workout is longer than A/B/C and that's the point. No upper body means more energy for legs and core. Use it — push the weights up, shorten the rest periods, stay focused. You're building the base for harder programming down the road.

Progressive Overload

The Rule
Step 1: Hit the TOP of the rep range on all sets (e.g., 3×12 when range is 10-12)
Step 2: Next session, add weight (+5 lbs lower body, +2.5 lbs upper body)
Step 3: Drop back to bottom of rep range with new weight
Step 4: Build back up to top of range. Repeat forever.
Realistic timeline: Weeks 1-4 = neurological adaptation. Weeks 8-12 = visible muscle changes. Months 3-6 = peak "newbie gains." ~0.5-1 lb of lean muscle per month.

Posture & Cervical Spine

Why this matters: Cervical spine dysfunction likely connects carpal tunnel AND tinnitus. The nerve glides are built into the warm-up. These extra exercises can be done on rest days or added after any workout.
Chin Tucks — 3 × 10, hold 5 sec Watch →
Pull chin straight back (double chin). Decompresses cervical spine. Do at desk hourly if possible.
Cervical Nerve Glides — 2 × 10 each side Watch →
Tilt head away from affected side, extend arm, open/close hand. Gently — never force into pain.
Prone Y-Raises — 3 × 12 Watch →
Face down on incline bench. Arms in Y, thumbs up. Lift with lower traps. Fixes forward head posture at the root.
Tinnitus connection: If the ringing changes when moving jaw or turning neck — that's somatosensory tinnitus. Cervical PT has shown significant improvement in studies.

Wrist Rehab Protocol

Bonus: The nerve glides are already baked into every warm-up above — so the rehab happens automatically. Doing them 2-3x daily (morning + before bed) would speed things up even more. Full protocol below.

Median Nerve Glides

5-10 REPS EACH HAND
1. Fist — wrist neutral, fingers and thumb curled Watch →
2. Fingers straight — wrist neutral (table-top)
3. Fingers and wrist extended back
4. Same + thumb extended out
5. Same + forearm supinated (palm up)
6. Same + gentle opposite-hand stretch on thumb
Flow through 1→6 slowly. Never force into pain.

Tendon Glides

5-10 REPS
1. Straight fingers (full extension) Watch →
2. Hook fist (bend at middle + end joints, base straight)
3. Full fist
4. Table-top (bend at base joints only)
5. Straight fist (bend at base + middle joints)
Hold each position 3-5 seconds. Smooth transitions.
Night splints: Wear neutral-position wrist splints every night. Cochrane review confirmed they improve CT symptoms. Use wrist wraps (not splints) during workouts.

Sleep Protocol

70% of growth hormone is released during deep sleep. Fixing sleep is directly tied to muscle gain at 38.

Lion's Mane
500-1,000 mg/day
Fruiting body extract, standardized. Take with dinner. Works through anxiety/stress reduction and nerve growth factor. Start at 500 mg, titrate to 1,000 mg over 2 weeks.

Evidence is moderate for cognition (Mori 2009), weak for sleep specifically — try 8 weeks; drop if no clear effect.
Magnesium Glycinate
200-400 mg before bed
Activates parasympathetic nervous system, regulates melatonin, binds GABA receptors. Start at 200 mg, work up to 400 mg.
Ashwagandha (KSM-66)
300-600 mg with dinner
Root extract standardized to 5% withanolides. Reduces cortisol ~20% at 60 days — less muscle breakdown, better recovery.

Wait until thyroid panel is back (TSH + Free T4 + Free T3 + TPO antibodies). Ashwagandha mildly stimulates thyroid and can flare Hashimoto's — given the family history, gate it behind the bloodwork in the Health Action Plan.
Optional Additions
L-Theanine + Tart Cherry
L-Theanine: 200 mg before bed. Alpha brain waves, no grogginess.
Tart cherry juice: 8 oz before bed. Natural melatonin + anti-inflammatory.
Start one at a time. Add each supplement 1 week apart. Lion's mane first → magnesium week 2 → ashwagandha week 3.

Anti-Inflammatory Nutrition (CT Support)

Add to Diet
Omega-3s: Salmon 2x/week, walnuts, flaxseed. If supplementing: 1-2 g/day combined EPA+DHA, EPA-dominant for mood and inflammation. ALA from walnuts/flax converts to EPA at <10% efficiency, so a fish-oil cap is the easier target hit.
Turmeric/curcumin: 500-1,000 mg/day with black pepper
Vitamin B6: 5-10 mg/day from a B-complex is enough. Do not exceed 12 mg/day of supplemental B6 long-term — sensory neuropathy reported above this in the EFSA 2023 re-evaluation. Get B6 from food first (chicken, salmon, potatoes, bananas).
Vitamin D: 2,000-4,000 IU/day if deficient
Alpha-lipoic acid: 300-600 mg/day
Zinc — read this BEFORE buying
Zinc dose belongs to the health-plan side. Do NOT start zinc supplementation until the bloodwork (serum zinc + serum copper + ceruloplasmin) is back. The copper IUD shifts the Cu:Zn ratio individually, and the IOM upper limit is 40 mg/day total — the right dose is whatever the panel says. Full context in the Health Action Plan §8.
Reduce / Avoid
Refined sugar + processed foods — promote inflammation
Excess sodium — increases fluid retention → more carpal tunnel swelling
Excessive alcohol — neurotoxic, disrupts sleep

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Gripping too hard
If hands are white-knuckled, you're compressing the median nerve. Versa Gripps + wraps every session.
Skipping the workout because wrists hurt
Every workout has 5+ exercises that need zero grip. Drop the 2 upper body moves if wrists are bad — the rest of the session is still a full workout.
Not eating enough
You can't build muscle in a deficit. 2,350 calories is the target. See the Eating Guide.
Expecting overnight results
Visible changes start week 8-12. Peak newbie gains months 3-6. Realistic year 1: 8-10 lbs of lean muscle.

Key Tips
