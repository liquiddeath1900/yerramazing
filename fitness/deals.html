His New Stack — D3, Magnesium, UC-II

Three additions to the skin + joint stack. Buy these once, they last 2-6 months.

Best Value
NOW Foods Vitamin D3 5000 IU
~$13.49 for 240 softgels — ~6 month supply at 1/day. NSF-certified facility, no fillers. Take with breakfast that has fat (avo, eggs) for absorption. Pilz 2011: D3 supports T levels in deficient men. Find on Amazon — Subscribe & Save eligible.
Best Value
Doctor's Best Magnesium Glycinate
~$14.99 for 240 tablets — ~2 month supply at 2/day (400mg). Chelated form (TRAACS) = no laxative effect, full absorption. PM with dinner for sleep + recovery. Avoid magnesium oxide (cheap = unabsorbed). On Amazon.
Best Value
NOW Foods UC-II Undenatured Type II Collagen 40mg
~$22.99 for 60 capsules — ~2 month supply at 1/day. Lugo 2016: UC-II at 40mg/day beat glucosamine 1500mg + chondroitin 1200mg head-to-head for knee function. Take on empty stomach (better absorption). On Amazon.
Don't get scammed by "multi-collagen" blends. "Type II collagen" listed in 5-type collagen powders is hydrolyzed and denatured — it does NOT do what UC-II does. UC-II works because it stays *undenatured* and triggers oral tolerance via gut Peyer's patches (Lugo 2016, Crowley 2009). The mechanism dies the moment heat or acid hydrolyzes it. Look for the trademarked "UC-II" branded ingredient on the label — anything else is marketing.

Aldi Money Hacks

Hack
Wednesday = New Ad Day
Aldi's new weekly ad starts Wednesday. Hit the store Wednesday morning for best selection on Aldi Finds (limited stock items like protein bars, collagen powder, meal prep containers).
Hack
Buy Produce in Season
Mangos, berries, avocados — prices swing hard by season. Buy frozen berries year-round (same nutrition, way cheaper). Mangos are cheapest April-August. Stock up on Aldi's frozen fruit blends for shakes.
Hack
Family Pack Chicken = Best Price
Aldi's family pack chicken breast is usually $1.99-2.49/lb — cheaper than any other grocery store. Buy 2 packs, freeze one. Same goes for ground turkey. Thighs are even cheaper ($1.49/lb).
Hack
Aldi Brands to Trust
  • Friendly Farms Greek Yogurt — same as Chobani, half the price
  • Simply Nature Oats — same as Quaker, $2 less
  • Happy Farms Eggs — $2-3/dozen, quality is fine
  • Earth Grown — their plant-based line (if she wants plant options)
  • Elevation Protein Bars — when in stock, $5 for a box of 4 (vs $12 for Quest)
